## 🚀 Installation Guide

### First-Time Installation

1. Head over to the **[Latest Release](https://github.com/shreekunal/Everthing.OS-Releases/releases/latest)** page.
2. Under the **Assets** section, download the `Everything-v1.0.xxx.apk` file.
3. Open the downloaded `.apk` on your Android device.
4. If prompted, enable **"Allow from this source"** in your device's *Install Unknown Apps* settings.
5. Tap **Install** and launch Everything OS.

## 🔄 Seamless In-App Auto-Updates

You do **not** need to manually visit this page for future updates!

Everything OS comes with a native in-app auto-updater.

### How to Update

1. Open **Everything OS**.
2. Navigate to **Settings → App Updates & Build**.
3. Tap **Check Updates**.
4. When a new build is detected, tap **Download & Install Update**.
5. The app downloads the APK and prompts the Android installer directly on your device.
