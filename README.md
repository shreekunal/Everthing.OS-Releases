    ## 🚀 Installation Guide                                                                                       
                                                                                                                   
    ### First-Time Installation
    1. Head over to the **[Latest Release](https://github.com/shreekunal/Everthing.OS-Releases/releases/latest)**  
    page.
    2. Under the **Assets** section, download the `Everything-v1.0.xxx.apk` file.
    3. Open the downloaded `.apk` on your Android device.
    4. If prompted, toggle **"Allow from this source"** in your device's *Install Unknown Apps* settings.          
    5. Tap **Install** and launch Everything OS.

    ## 🔄 Seamless In-App Auto-Updates                                                                             
                                                                                                                   
    You do **not** need to manually visit this page for future updates!                                            
                                                                                                                   
    Everything OS comes with a native in-app auto-updater:                                                         
    - Navigate to **Settings** → **App Updates & Build**.                                                          
    - Tap **Check Updates**.                                                                                       
    - When a new build is detected, tap **Download & Install Update** — the app downloads and prompts the installer
    directly on your device.
